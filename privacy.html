Snowfall Village - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Snowfall Village is made by We Make Games Ltd.

The game does not collect, store, sell or share any personal information. It has no adverts, no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it does not need an internet connection to play (only buying or restoring the full game goes through Google Play).

The first chapter is free. If you buy the full game, the purchase is handled entirely by Google Play, under Google's own privacy policy. We never see your name, your payment details or your Google account; the game only asks Google Play whether the full game has been bought on this account, so it can unlock it and restore it on a new phone.

Your progress (levels, stars, Daily Puzzle streaks, the Village Album and your wardrobe) and your settings (sound, music and vibration) are saved only on your own device. They are deleted if you uninstall the game, and you can also wipe your progress at any time from Settings > Reset progress. Resetting progress does not remove the full-game purchase.

The game uses the phone's vibration only if you leave the Vibration setting switched on.
